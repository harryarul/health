Pillar — Nutrition

The simple pattern.

Forget perfect. A mostly-plant, mostly-whole-food pattern, most of the time, does almost all the work.

  • Plants firstFill half your plate with vegetables and fruit. Colour and fibre are the goal — variety feeds a healthier gut.
  • Whole over refinedChoose oats, brown rice, whole wheat and quinoa over white, processed versions for slower, steadier energy.
  • Healthy fatsOlive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado and oily fish — over butter, fried food and ultra-processed snacks.
  • Protein at each mealBeans, lentils, fish, eggs, yoghurt or lean meat keep you full and protect muscle as you age.
  • Go easy on ultra-processedThe biggest lever isn't a superfood — it's cooking more and leaning on packaged food less.
