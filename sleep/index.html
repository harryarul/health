Pillar — Sleep

Why it matters more than you think.

Sleep isn't lost time. It's active, nightly maintenance for nearly every part of you.

  • It repairs your bodyDeep sleep is when tissues mend, hormones rebalance, and blood pressure and heart rate dip and recover.
  • It clears your mindSleep consolidates memory and emotion. Short nights blunt focus, mood and decision-making.
  • It steadies appetiteToo little sleep nudges hunger hormones the wrong way, making cravings harder to resist.
  • Consistency is kingA regular wake time — even on weekends — anchors your body clock more than anything else.
Sleep hygiene

Small tweaks, deeper sleep.

🌡️

Cool & dark

A cool, dark, quiet room helps you fall and stay asleep. Blackout and a fan or earplugs help.

📵

Screens down

Dim lights and put screens away ~60 min before bed. Bright light tells your brain it's daytime.

☕

Mind your timing

Keep caffeine to the morning and big meals or alcohol away from bedtime.

🔁

Wind-down ritual

A short, repeatable routine — shower, stretch, read — signals your body it's time to rest.
