Pillar — Hydration

Why water is the easy win.

Every cell in you depends on it, most of us run a little low, and the fix is wonderfully simple.

  • It runs everythingWater carries nutrients, cushions joints, regulates temperature and helps your heart pump efficiently.
  • A little low is commonMild dehydration is easy to miss — it can show up as tiredness, headaches or poor concentration.
  • Check the colourPale, straw-coloured urine usually means you're well hydrated; dark means top up.
  • Needs varyHeat, exercise, illness and pregnancy raise your needs. Thirst is a useful guide — listen to it.
Easy wins

Simple ways to drink more.

🥤

Keep it visible

A bottle on your desk or in your bag is the single best nudge to drink more.

⏰

Anchor to habits

A glass with each meal, on waking, and after the bathroom adds up fast.

🍓

Eat your water

Cucumber, watermelon, oranges, soups and yoghurt all top up your fluids.

🍋

Make it nicer

Bored of plain water? Add lemon, mint, berries or a splash of juice.

Your rough daily water target
