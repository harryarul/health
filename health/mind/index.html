Pillar — Mind & stress

Everyday resets.

Stress is normal — staying stuck in it isn't. These small, repeatable practices keep your nervous system resilient.

🫁

Breathe

Slow, long exhales switch on your body's calm response in moments. Try the tool below.

🚶

Move

Even a short walk — ideally outdoors — is one of the most reliable mood lifters there is.

🌳

Get outside

Time in nature and daylight lowers stress and helps your sleep and mood.

🤝

Connect

A real conversation with someone you trust is protective for mental health.

📴

Unplug

Short, deliberate breaks from screens and news lower background stress.

🙏

Notice the good

A few seconds of genuine gratitude, daily, gently retrains attention.

When to reach out

Self-care is powerful, but it isn't everything. If low mood, anxiety or stress is lasting more than a couple of weeks, affecting your sleep, work or relationships, or you just feel you're not coping — that's a good reason to talk to someone. A doctor or mental-health professional can help, and reaching out early is a sign of strength, not weakness. If you ever feel unsafe or in crisis, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line in your country right away.

Try it

A 4-4-4-4 reset.

Guided practice

Box breathing

Breathe in, hold, out, hold — four counts each. Follow the circle for a minute or two whenever you feel wound up.
