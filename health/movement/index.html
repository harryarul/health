Pillar — Movement

The weekly recipe.

You don't need a gym. A simple mix of aerobic work, a little strength, and everyday movement does the heavy lifting.

🚶
150m

Aerobic base

Moderate activity each week — brisk walking, cycling, swimming. Roughly 30 min, 5 days.

💪
2×

Strength

Two sessions a week working all major muscle groups — bodyweight, bands or weights.

🧘
10m

Mobility & calm

A little daily stretching, yoga or breathing keeps you supple and lowers stress.

🐾
8k

Move often

Steps and fidgets add up. Break up long sitting with a minute of movement each hour.

Make it stick

Consistency is the whole game.

  • Start where you areFive minutes counts. The best exercise is the one you'll actually repeat tomorrow.
  • Make it enjoyableWalk with a friend, dance, garden, play. Adherence beats intensity every time.
  • Build graduallyAdd a few minutes or a little load each week. Small, steady steps protect you from injury.
  • Warm up, cool downEase in and out. Stop for chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness — and see a doctor if it persists.
