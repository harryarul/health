A Whole You pillar

Lifestyle is
powerful medicine.

How you eat, move, breathe, and think can prevent, manage, and in some cases reverse the diseases that frighten us most. This pillar gathers the evidence — and shows you how to use it alongside, never instead of, good medical care.

The big idea

In the Mount Abu Open Heart Trial, people with documented coronary artery disease followed a programme of low-fat plant-based eating, gentle exercise, and daily meditation. The patients who stuck with it most closely showed measurable regression of the plaque narrowing their arteries, and had fewer cardiac events. The body, given the right conditions, repairs itself.

Our stance

This pillar takes a natural-first but responsible view. Lifestyle deserves to be treated as front-line medicine, because the evidence says it works. But that is a reason to do more, not to do less of what keeps you safe.

!

This is education, not medical advice. Everything here is meant to work alongside your doctor, not replace them.

Never start, stop, or change a prescribed medication on your own. If you want to lean harder into lifestyle, that's a conversation to have with your prescriber — many will happily help you reduce medication as your numbers improve.
